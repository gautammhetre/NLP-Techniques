## FINAL PROMPT (Copy & Paste Ready for Midjourney/DALL-E/Flux)

```
Create a premium, modern Meta feed ad image (1080x1350 pixels) for a modular kitchen furniture brand called "The Wooden Home."

LAYOUT & COMPOSITION:
- Top 40%: Showcase a sleek, modern acrylic modular kitchen with open tandem drawers displaying organized crockery, utensils, and smart storage solutions. The kitchen features a loft storage unit with clean geometric lines. Warm professional lighting (natural daylight aesthetic). Minimalist backdrop with soft gray-white walls and light wood flooring. Kitchen appears spacious, uncluttered, and expertly designed. Premium product photography style.

- Middle 25%: Display a vibrant festival offer badge (lime green rounded rectangle) with white bold text reading "FESTIVAL OFFER" and brown subtext "₹1,25,000 /- onwards". Position centrally or top-right with subtle shadow. Below this, add 3 benefit cards in a grid:
  * Icon + "Acrylic Finish" + lime green underline
  * Icon + "Smart Storage" + lime green underline
  * Icon + "Premium Fitting" + lime green underline
  Each with simple modern line-style icons (white fill, brown outline).

- Bottom 35%: CTA section with lime green (#c0d72d) background or gradient fade from white to lime. Include:
  * Bold white headline text: "Transform Your Kitchen Today"
  * Contact information in large brown text: "Call: 7020465583" or "WhatsApp: 7020465583"
  * Availability text: "Available in Pune | Nashik"
  * White arrow or button element pointing to contact details

BRAND INTEGRATION:
- Top-left or top-center: The Wooden Home logo (lime green "W" arrow symbol with brown text "THE WOODEN HOME" below). Rendered in premium, minimalist style.
- Overall brand positioning: Premium but accessible, modern, trustworthy, space-conscious, design-focused.

COLOR PALETTE (Critical - Brand Colors):
- Primary: Lime/Neon Green (#c0d72d) - use for offer badge, accents, key highlights, CTA buttons
- Secondary: Warm Brown (#8B6F47) - use for brand name, premium text, shadows, borders
- Accent: White/Cream (#F5F5F5) - backgrounds, light text, breathing room
- Depth: Dark Olive (#5a5a3a) - shadows, premium depth, text hierarchy

KITCHEN VISUAL DETAILS:
- Acrylic finish with glossy sheen reflecting light beautifully
- Open tandem drawers at various heights showing organized interior
- Mix of white, cream, and light wood tones in the kitchen
- Crockery, utensils, and storage items visible but not cluttered
- Loft unit above with clean, minimalist design
- Stainless steel premium fittings catching light
- Professional, high-end product photography quality
- Bright, aspirational feel (not dark or moody)
- Shot appears professional, like Behance/Design Magazine quality

TYPOGRAPHY & TEXT:
- Main headline (Festival Offer): Bold, large, sans-serif, white on lime green (40-50px equivalent)
- Subheader (Price): Clean sans-serif, brown (#8B6F47), (28-32px equivalent)
- Benefit text: Light sans-serif, brown, small (14-16px equivalent)
- CTA text: Bold sans-serif, white on lime green, (24-28px equivalent)
- All text must be legible on mobile (readable at 50% size)

STYLE & MOOD:
- Modern, premium, professional yet warm and inviting
- Aspirational but not unattainable (premium accessibility positioning)
- Trustworthy, sophisticated, design-conscious
- Urgency implied (festival offer visual treatment)
- Emphasis on innovation (smart storage, tandem drawers)
- Focus on transformation (before/after implicit)

TECHNICAL SPECS:
- Dimensions: 1080x1350 pixels (Instagram/Meta vertical feed format)
- Render quality: 4K/Ultra HD (can be downscaled)
- No people, faces, or models (product-focused)
- Sharp focus on kitchen design and details
- Professional product photography, not stock photo
- Rendered 3D visualization style OR professional photography hybrid
- Suitable for Meta/Facebook feed placement

AVOID:
- Cluttered or busy layouts
- Colors outside the specified palette
- Generic or cheap-looking fonts
- People/faces in the image
- Offer badge that's too small or illegible
- JavaScript-rendered or static mockup appearance
- Dark moody lighting (bright and aspirational instead)
- Watermarks or logos not specified in brand section
```
