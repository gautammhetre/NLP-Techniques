## LANGUAGE STRATEGY: ENGLISH vs. MARATHI

### Keep in ENGLISH (Technical/SEO Terms)
- **Kitchen** - more searchable than रसोई
- **Modular Kitchen** - industry standard, widely searched
- **Design** - higher SEO value in English
- **Acrylic** - technical term, English standard
- **Smart Storage** - compound technical term
- **Interior Design Ideas** - professional English phrase
- **Premium Quality** - credible in luxury context
- **Hardware** - technical standard
- **Remodeling** - industry terminology
- **Consultation** - professional term

### Keep in MARATHI (Emotional/Trust Terms)
- **घरासाठी** (for your home) - emotional resonance
- **निशुल्क** (complimentary/free) - trust signal
- **तज्ञ सल्ला** (expert consultation) - credibility
- **ब्रँडेड** (branded) - local trust indicator
- **वॉरंटी** (warranty) - assurance
- **मुफ्त** (free) - cultural emphasis
- **आजच** (today) - urgency in native language
